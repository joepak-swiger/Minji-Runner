## Invincibility music

- Asset: **Invincible!**
- Runtime file used in Gold v0.6: `assets/audio/invincible.mp3`
- Original preserved in project: `assets/audio/invincible.wav`
- Creator: **NBJDLukasAbsolute**
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/invincible

This track plays on a separate music layer specifically while Minji has fruit-based invulnerability. The regular background track continues underneath it at a reduced volume. Gold v0.6 uses an MP3 transcode for mobile compatibility while retaining the supplied WAV.
